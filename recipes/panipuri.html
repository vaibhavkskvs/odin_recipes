Panipuri


To make panipuri, prepare the spicy mint water by blending fresh mint, coriander, green chilies, ginger, lemon juice, roasted cumin, and spices with water. Make the filling by mixing boiled and mashed potatoes with cooked chickpeas, salt, and spices. Finally, crack a small hole in each puri, fill it with the potato mixture, add a little sweet tamarind chutney, and dip it into the chilled spicy pani before serving immediately.


Description

    Ingredients

      For the Pani
      • Fresh mint leaves: 1 cup
      • Fresh coriander leaves: ½ cup
      • Green chilies: 2-3
      • Ginger: 1 tsp
      • Roasted cumin powder: 1 tsp
      • Chaat masala: 1 tsp
      • Black salt: ½ tsp
      • Lemon juice: 1-2 tbsp
      • Water: 4 cups
      • Salt: To taste

      For the Filling
      • Boiled potatoes: 3 medium
      • Cooked chickpeas: ½ cup
      • Red chili powder: ½ tsp
      • Chaat masala: 1 tsp
      • Salt: To taste
      • Fresh coriander leaves: 1 tbsp, chopped

      For Serving
      • Panipuri puris: 20-25
      • Sweet tamarind chutney: ½ cup
      • Spicy pani: Prepared above

    Steps

      Make the Pani
      1. Add mint leaves, coriander leaves, green chilies, and ginger to a blender.
      2. Add a little water and blend everything into a smooth paste.
      3. Transfer the paste to a large bowl and add roasted cumin powder, chaat masala, black salt, lemon juice, and salt.
      4. Add the remaining water and mix everything thoroughly.
      5. Taste the pani and adjust the salt, spices, and lemon juice as needed.
      6. Refrigerate the pani until it is well chilled.
      Make the Filling
      1. Peel and mash the boiled potatoes in a bowl.
      2. Add cooked chickpeas, red chili powder, chaat masala, and salt.
      3. Add chopped coriander leaves and mix everything together thoroughly.
      Assemble the Panipuri
      1. Take one puri and gently make a small hole on the top using your thumb or finger.
      2. Fill the puri with a small amount of the potato and chickpea mixture.
      3. Add a little sweet tamarind chutney inside the puri.
      4. Dip the filled puri into the chilled spicy pani.
      5. Serve immediately and repeat with the remaining puris.
