Samosa


To make samosas, start by rubbing 2 cups flour, ¼ cup ghee/oil, and 1 tsp salt together, then knead with ⅓ cup water into a firm dough and let it rest for 20 minutes. Next, make the filling by sautéing cumin seeds, ginger, and green chilies, then adding ½ cup peas, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp garam masala, 1 tsp amchur, and 4 boiled, mashed potatoes before letting it cool. Finally, roll the dough into ovals, cut them in half, fold each semicircle into a cone, stuff with the potato mixture, seal the edges with water, and fry on low heat for 10 to 15 minutes until golden brown.


Description

    Ingredients

      For the dough
      • Flour (maida): 2 cups
      • Ghee or oil: ¼ cup (melted)
      • Salt: 1 tsp
      • Water: ⅓ cup(or as needed to knead a stiff dough)

      For the Potato Filling
      • Boiled potatoes: 3-4 medium
      • Green peas: ½ cup (boiled or frozen)
      • Oil or ghee: 1-2 tbsp
      • Cumin seeds: ½ tsp
      • Ginger: 1 tsp finely chopped or paste
      • Green chilies: 1-2, chopped
      • Coriander powder: ½ to 1 tsp
      • Garam masala: ½ tsp
      • Salt: To taste
      • Fresh coriander leaves: 1 tbsp, chopped
      • Oil: For deep frying

    Steps

      Make the Dough
      1. Mix the flour, salt, and carom seeds in a large bowl.
      2. Add the melted ghee or oil. Rub it into the flour with your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
      3. Add water slowly and knead into a firm, stiff dough. Do not make it soft.
      4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 20 to 30 minutes.
      Make the Filling
      1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle.
      2. Add chopped ginger and green chilies. Sauté for a few seconds.
      3. Add green peas, coriander powder, garam masala, salt, and amchur powder. Stir well.
      4. Add the boiled, coarsely mashed potatoes. Mix everything together thoroughly.
      5. Turn off the heat, mix in fresh coriander leaves, and let the filling cool down completely.
      Shape the Samosas
      1. Divide the rested dough into equal small balls. Roll one ball into a thin oval shape.
      2. Cut the oval straight across the middle to make two equal semicircles.
      3. Take one semicircle and dab a little water along the straight edge.
      4. Fold the straight edges together to form a cone shape, and pinch the seam firmly to seal it.
      5. Fill the cone with 1.5 to 2 tablespoons of the potato mixture. Do not overfill.
      6. Apply a little water around the top inner edge, and press the edges together to seal the samosa closed. Repeat with the rest of the dough.
      Fry the Samosas
      1. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over low-to-medium heat. The oil should not be too hot, or the crust will bubble and stay raw inside.
      2. Gently slide the prepared samosas into the warm oil.
      3. Fry on medium-low heat, turning occasionally, until they turn golden brown and crispy (about 10 to 15 minutes).
      4. Remove them with a slotted spoon and drain excess oil on paper towels.
      5. Serve hot with green mint chutney and sweet tamarind chutney.
